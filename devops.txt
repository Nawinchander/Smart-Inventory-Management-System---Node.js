This workflow structure is close to what is used in SDE-2/Senior Backend and DevOps environments at Amazon, Uber, Walmart, Netflix, and PayPal.


Developer
    ↓
git push
    ↓
CI
    ↓
Security Scan
    ↓
SonarQube
    ↓
Load Test
    ↓
Deploy to Staging
    ↓
Manual Approval
    ↓
Production Deployment
    ↓
Monitoring (Prometheus)
    ↓
Grafana
    ↓
Logging (ELK)
    ↓
Rollback if Failure
